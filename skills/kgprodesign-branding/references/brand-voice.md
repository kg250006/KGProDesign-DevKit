# KG ProDesign — Brand Voice & Messaging

<tone>

## Brand Tone

- **Professional** — Credible, competent, trustworthy
- **Modern** — Current technology, forward-thinking approach
- **Confident** — Decisive, experienced (since 2005), proven track record
- **Approachable** — No jargon walls, collaborative, partner-oriented

### Do
- Speak with authority backed by experience
- Be direct and clear
- Focus on outcomes and solutions
- Use active voice

### Don't
- Use corporate buzzwords ("synergy", "leverage", "paradigm shift")
- Be overly casual or use slang
- Make vague promises
- Use passive voice when active is possible

</tone>
